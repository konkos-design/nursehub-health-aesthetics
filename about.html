Care you can trust, close to home

NurseHub is an independent, nurse-led clinic run by Melissa Hammond, an Advanced Nurse Practitioner with more than 20 years of nursing behind her.

Why a nurse-led clinic

An Advanced Nurse Practitioner is trained to assess, diagnose, prescribe, order and interpret tests, and refer you to specialist services when needed.

Melissa started NurseHub so people in and around Newtownmountkennedy could get timely, compassionate care without long waits. The same clinical thinking shapes the aesthetic side: honest advice, treatments tailored to you rather than trends, and results that enhance rather than change.

Patient safety comes first in everything, from a sore throat on a Sunday to a course of skin boosters.

  • Advanced Nurse PractitionerRegistered to assess, diagnose and treat a wide range of conditions
  • Nurse PrescriberCan prescribe for minor illness when clinically appropriate
  • Finalist, Irish Aesthetics & Enhancement Awards 2026Nurse Practitioner category
  • Professional Certificate in Surgical SkillsDublin City University
  • ICN NP/APN Network Conference 2026Represented Irish Advanced Nurse Practitioners in Nashville
  • Workplace Wellness pilotPreventive screening for local businesses with Project Health

Our mission

To provide compassionate, professional and reliable nursing care that improves quality of life for individuals and families in the community, with better local access to health care.

Our vision

To be a leading community-based health care provider, bringing high-quality, patient-centred care closer to home through the leadership of Advanced Nurse Practitioners.
