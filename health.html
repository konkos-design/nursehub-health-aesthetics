Cardiovascular screening

Full screening€200

A health assessment that looks at your risk of heart disease, stroke and other problems with the heart and blood vessels, often before any symptoms appear.

  • Medical history and physical examination
  • Blood tests
  • Rhythm strip
  • 24-hour blood pressure monitor

You leave with a clear report and advice on lifestyle, medication or further investigations.

24-hour blood pressure monitoring

Including review€100

A small cuff and recorder you wear for a day and a night while you go about normal life. It shows what your blood pressure really does outside the clinic.

  • Diagnose high blood pressure
  • Spot white-coat or masked hypertension
  • Check how well your medication is working
  • Track changes overnight
